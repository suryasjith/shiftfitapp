## 2. Physical Activity Readiness Questionnaire (PAR-Q Standard)

Before utilizing ShiftFit for resistance training or high-intensity exercise, review the following standard clinical safety screening questions:

1. Has your doctor ever said that you have a heart condition and that you should only participate in physical activity recommended by a doctor?
2. Do you feel pain in your chest when you engage in physical activity?
3. In the past month, have you had chest pain when you were not doing physical activity?
4. Do you lose your balance because of dizziness or do you ever lose consciousness?
5. Do you have a bone or joint problem (for example, back, knee, or hip) that could be made worse by a change in your physical activity?
6. Is your doctor currently prescribing medication for your blood pressure or heart condition?
7. Do you know of any other reason why you should not engage in physical activity?

**If you answered YES to any of these questions, seek medical clearance from a licensed physician prior to initiating physical training.**

---

## 3. Emergency Cessation Protocols

Immediately stop training and seek medical attention if you experience:
- Sudden chest pain, tightness, or pressure
- Acute dizziness, lightheadedness, or loss of balance
- Unusual shortness of breath or irregular heartbeat
- Sharp, stabbing musculoskeletal or joint pain
- Nausea, cold sweats, or sudden visual disturbances
